Tate Fine Art Photography: four directions

Home page and one story page per direction, built with her own photographs (hot-linked from the public dataset) and draft copy in her voice. Shots are the top of each page at 390, 768 and 1280; open a page to see the whole thing. Details, references and the divergence table are in README.md.

BetVisitors from a credit want to scan everything she has made and judge the eye by the edit.

CostsDense on a phone; needs six good frames per story; more archive than feeling.
