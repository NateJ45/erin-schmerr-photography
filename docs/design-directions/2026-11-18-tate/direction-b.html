The photographer

It started with my dad's camera.

My dad was a self-proclaimed picture nerd, never without a camera around his neck. Years after he died, I finally turned his camera on again and found the last photo he ever took: fall leaves, from our kitchen window.

Through his lens I fell in love with photography. I've spent years trying to perfect the look of an authentic vintage photograph, which means doing almost everything opposite to a modern shoot.

I never prioritize beauty standards. I want to capture a person's essence.From my interview with CanvasRebel

Most of my sessions start from a story, a decade or a film. I shoot around Cincinnati, in places like the American Sign Museum and Mid Century Modern Rescue, and I will always say yes to Halloween.
