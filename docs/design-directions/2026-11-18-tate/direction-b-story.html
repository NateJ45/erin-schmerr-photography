VINTAGE + HALLOWEEN, my two favorite things in one living room. Mid Century Modern Rescue gave us a living room that already looked the part, and four very good sports came in costume.

I wanted these to look like the photos you find in a shoebox years later. So the set is intentionally grainy, the shutter speed is turned down, and the images are overexposed in places. It's the way dad's old camera would have seen the party.

For every photo I take, I tend to edit multiple variations. Some of these went to black and white and stayed there.

Thank you to my cast for playing along, and to Mid Century Modern Rescue for the room.
