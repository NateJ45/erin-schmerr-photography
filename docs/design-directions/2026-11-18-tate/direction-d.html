Page 8 · About

It started with my dad's camera.

My dad was a self-proclaimed picture nerd, never without a camera around his neck. Years after he died, I finally turned his camera on again and found the last photo he ever took: fall leaves, from our kitchen window. Through his lens I fell in love with photography.
