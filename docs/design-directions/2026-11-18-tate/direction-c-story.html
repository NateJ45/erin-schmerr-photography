Chapter I · The 1950s

A Halloween at home

Grainy, overexposed in places, a little chaotic: the party as dad's old camera would have caught it.

Mid Century Modern Rescue, Cincinnati · October 2023

Scene one · The living room

A room that already looked the part, a glowing television, and a jack-o-lantern on every surface.

VINTAGE + HALLOWEEN. I wanted these to look like photos you find in a shoebox years later, so the set is intentionally grainy, the shutter speed is turned down, and the images are overexposed in places.

For every photo I take, I tend to edit multiple variations. Some of these went to black and white and stayed there.
